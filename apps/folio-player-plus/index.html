Folio Player Plus

Your films.Both screens.

The video player made for iPhone Duo. Open it and the film sits on one half while your library fills the other. Stand it up like a small laptop, and the picture rises to the top.

MKV, MP4 and MOV. HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision. Your own files and servers, in one simple player, with no subscription.

Features

The all-in-one player for the Duo.

01

Made for iPhone Duo

Folded or open, the app follows the screen. In laptop pose, picture on top and controls below.

02

Plays almost everything

HEVC and H.264 up to 4K, HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision. DTS and TrueHD sound converted on the fly.

03

Every source

Local files, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, SMB, WebDAV, SFTP and more, with offline downloads for the road.

04

Subtitles, your way

SRT, ASS, WebVTT, PGS and VobSub, or fetched in your language. AirPlay to your TV, subtitles included.
