Relief Player

Every film.In depth.

A video player for Apple Vision Pro that turns ordinary 2D films into live depth, in real time, on the device.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • Apple Vision Pro

A screen four metres wide. Curve it, move it, dim the room around it. Then watch your own films come out of the frame.

Features

Your films, in depth.

01

Live depth

Every frame is analysed on the Neural Engine and turned into depth as it plays, right on your Vision Pro.

02

Your screen, your way

Choose its size and curve, lock it in place, dim the room or let your surroundings show through.

03

Your library

Local files and folders, network shares and web links, MKV included. Plex, Jellyfin and Emby with Plus.

04

Subtitles and sound

SRT, WebVTT, ASS, PGS and VobSub, drawn right in front of the screen, flat or curved.

Your living room

A giant screen, wherever you sit.

Put on Apple Vision Pro and your film fills the wall, four metres wide.

Home

Pick up where you left off.

Continue watching and recently added, with the artwork and story of every title.

Library

Your whole collection, sorted.

Browse by folder, source or genre, and filter in a tap.

Sources

Every place your films live.

Files and folders, network shares and video links, side by side.
