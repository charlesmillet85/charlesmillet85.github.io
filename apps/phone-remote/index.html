Phone Remote

Your iPhone.Anywhere you look.

An open-source experiment that shows your iPhone's live screen in a window on Apple Vision Pro or Mac, and lets you use it from there.

View on GitHub
  • iPhone
  • Mac
  • Apple Vision Pro

No server, no account, no internet. Your devices find each other on your Wi-Fi and connect directly, encrypted with a pairing code.

Features

Your iPhone, in a window on Vision Pro or Mac.

01

Look and pinch

On Apple Vision Pro, look and pinch to tap, pinch and drag to swipe.

02

Click and type

On Mac, click to tap, scroll to swipe, and type or paste from your keyboard.

03

Private by design

Local Wi-Fi only, with TLS and a key derived from an 8-character pairing code.

04

Open source

Read the code, build it yourself. A personal project, provided as is.
