Features

Give your phone a personality.

01

13 personalities

Grumpy, sunny, very British, AI, needy, teenage, bossy, smooth, boomer, gym bro, posh, Karen and influencer. Male and female voices for most of them. Or let Shuffle pick a new one each time.

02

Set up in a minute

A guide, narrated by one of the characters, adds the two Shortcuts automations: one for unfolding, one for folding.

03

You decide when

Every time, or once every few hours. On unfold, fold or unlock. Only at the times of day you choose, at the volume you choose.

04

Free to try

The sunny iPhone and the gym bro in full, plus a taste of the grumpy one. Then $0.99 per character, or unlock everyone at once. No subscription, no ads, no account.

Questions.

It doesn't speak when I open my phone.

Open Hello Duo and follow the guide: it adds two Shortcuts automations, one for unfolding and one for folding. Settings shows a tick next to each once it has run. To hear it every time, choose “Every time” in Settings › When it speaks › How often.

Can I choose when it speaks?

Yes: how often, on unfold, fold or unlock, only at certain times of the day, and how loud.

Does it work on other iPhones?

It's made for iPhone Duo. On other iPhones, it speaks when you unlock.
