Hello Duo

Hello,Duo.

A charming little companion for your iPhone Duo. Open it, close it or unlock it, and it greets you with a playful comment, a cheeky remark or something unexpectedly sweet.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • iPhone

No chatbot. No pop-ups. Nothing on your screen. It waits quietly in the background, and just says its line when you open or close your Duo.

Features

Give your phone a personality.

01

10 personalities

Grumpy, sunny, very British, AI, needy, teenage, bossy, smooth, boomer and gym bro. Each with its own voice and lines written for the Duo.

02

You decide when

Every time, or once every few hours. On unfold, fold or unlock. Only at the times of day you choose.

03

Discreet and offline

Every line is recorded in advance and plays offline. No account, no ads, nothing collected.

04

Free to try

The sunny iPhone and the gym bro in full. One purchase unlocks everyone else, Shuffle, Shortcuts and the app icons. No subscription.

Questions.

It doesn't speak when I open my phone.

Switch a character on with the button on its card, and keep Hello Duo running in the background. By default it only speaks now and then: in Settings › When it speaks › How often, choose “Every time”.

Can I choose when it speaks?

Yes: how often, on unfold, fold or unlock, and only at certain times of the day (Settings › When it speaks).

Does it work on other iPhones?

It's made for iPhone Duo. On other iPhones, it speaks when you unlock.
